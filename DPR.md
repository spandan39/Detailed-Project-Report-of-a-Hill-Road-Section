# Detailed-Project-Report-of-a-Hill-Road-Section
Topographic survey, detailed design and cost estimation of a hill road section
The total length of the road is 2.55 km. The designed bituminous pavement thickness comprises of 200 mm Granular Subbase, 250 mm granular base and 50 mm Asphalt concrete. The total filling volume is 12129.24 cum and total cutting volume is 23502.55 cum. For the stability of road, Gabion Retaining Wall and Stone Masonry Breast wall are used with total volumes of 770 cu.m and 480.9264 cu.m respectively. The road has maximum gradient of percent 9.97% and minimum gradient of 0.7%. The work has been carried out through map study and topographic survey. Design has been carried out using SW Road software. Pavement design has been conducted considering ordinary soil sample.
